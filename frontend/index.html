✦ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. Still confused? WhatsApp us directly.

Ask on WhatsApp
What is IELTS and why should I prepare for it? ▼
IELTS (International English Language Testing System) is the world's most popular English proficiency test accepted by 11,000+ organisations in 140+ countries. It is required for immigration, university admissions, and many professional registrations abroad.
What is the minimum age to join? ▼
Students from age 12 onwards can join our Spoken English and Grammar Foundation courses. IELTS preparation batches are recommended for students 16 years and above.
Are online classes available? ▼
Yes! We offer both offline (at our Begum Bagh center) and online classes via Zoom/Google Meet. Online students receive all study materials digitally and the same quality of instruction.
How long is the IELTS course? ▼
Our standard IELTS preparation course is 12 weeks (3 months). We also offer intensive 4-week crash courses for students who have already attempted IELTS and want to improve their band score.
What is the fee structure? ▼
Fee varies by course and batch type. Please contact us on +91 99977 56675 or WhatsApp for current fee details, batch schedules, and available discounts for early enrollment.
Do you offer OET and Spanish courses too? ▼
Yes! We offer OET (Occupational English Test) preparation for healthcare professionals targeting UK, Australia, and New Zealand, along with beginner and intermediate Spanish language courses.
Is study material included in the fee? ▼
Absolutely. All study materials — books, worksheets, practice tests, and vocabulary notes — are provided as part of the course. No additional purchase is required.
